## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand. Design a distinctive professional logo symbol for QAtron, a software testing application. A bold continuous dark forest-green ribbon bends into an open squared spiral with softened corners, suggesting a lowercase q and a traversable test path. The ribbon ends at lower right with one pale-lime segment. Clever negative space, asymmetric engineered geometry, very few broad shapes, polished Swiss identity design. Single symbol only, centered, fills 85 percent of a square. Perfectly flat uniform colors: #214d43 and #d7edaa on a completely solid warm ivory #fffefa background. Absolutely no texture, no grain, no gradients, no shading, no 3D, no holes through the solid ribbon, no glow, no tiny details. Smooth clean vector-style edges. No text, no frame, no checkmark, magnifying glass, shield, robot, sparkle or watermark. Make the mark beautiful and readable at 32px.
